## How to Redeem Codes in Heartopia

Redeeming codes in Heartopia is straightforward and accessible right from the start of your journey:

1. **Open the In-Game Menu**: Click on the **Clock / Watch icon** in the top-right corner of your screen (or press `Tab` on PC).
2. **Navigate to Settings**: Click the Settings gear icon from the navigation grid.
3. **Select General Settings**: In the left sidebar, tap on the **General** tab.
4. **Tap Redeem Code**: Locate the **Redeem Code** button at the bottom-right corner of the window.
5. **Input and Confirm**: Enter or paste your working code and press Confirm.
6. **Collect from Mailbox**: Head outside your house and check your mailbox to collect all claimed rewards.

## Pro Troubleshooting & Code Expiry Protocols

1. **Case Sensitivity**: Codes must match capitalization exactly (e.g., `OCTOBERWEEK2`, not `octoberweek2`).
2. **Space Stripping**: When copying on mobile devices, trailing spaces often get copied automatically, causing a "Code Invalid" error. Trim any whitespace before submitting.
3. **Mailbox Expiration Timer**: Once redeemed, items sit in your front-yard mailbox for **30 calendar days**. Make sure to empty your inbox so valuable lures and fertilizers do not expire.
