## Pro Donation Rules & Inventory Management

Having cataloged every wing on live servers, here is the optimal protocol to prevent accidental loss of ultra-rare specimens:

### 1. The First Catch Protocol
Before listing any item on the market square to Albert Jr. or tossing it into kitchen stoves, check your item card. If the **Museum Stamp** icon is greyed out, lock the item in your bag and visit Curator Barnaby immediately.

### 2. Star Quality Independence
A common beginner misconception is waiting for a 5-star specimen before donating. **The Museum does not track star ratings.** A 1-star Storm Marlin completes the display just as effectively as a 5-star catch. Save your 5-star specimens for the [Heartopia Gold Making Guide](/guides/gold-making-guide/) to cash in on maximum selling prices!

### 3. Golden Tools Durability & Buffs
- **Golden Fishing Rod**: Never snaps lines during tension spikes; adds +50% reel recovery speed.
- **Golden Bug Net**: Doubles swing radius; zero noise footprint during stealth approaches.
- **Golden Watering Can**: Waters a 5x5 grid (25 tiles) in a single press.
- **Golden Pickaxe**: Smashes all rock and crystal veins in 2 hits regardless of hardness rating.
