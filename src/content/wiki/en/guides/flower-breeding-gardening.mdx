## Fundamentals of Gardening & Crossbreeding

Gardening is both a peaceful pastime and an essential crafting supply chain in Heartopia. Beyond common crops like wheat and carrots, **Flower Breeding** allows you to cultivate rare hybrid colors (such as Purple Tulips, Black Roses, and Gold Lilies) for high-end boutique dyes and decorative displays.

## Optimal Checkerboard Planting Layout

To maximize hybrid sprout chances, arrange parent flowers in a **diagonal checkerboard pattern**:

```
[ P ]  [ . ]  [ P ]
[ . ]  [ H ]  [ . ]
[ P ]  [ . ]  [ P ]
```
*Legend: `[ P ]` = Parent flower | `[ . ]` = Empty tilled soil | `[ H ]` = Potential hybrid bud*

- Leaving empty adjacent spaces gives new hybrid seeds room to germinate.
- Always water parent flowers daily until you see glistening droplets. Unwatered flowers will not reproduce.

## Increasing Bloom Star Ratings (1⭐ to 5⭐)

Every flower species possesses a quality rating ranging from 1 to 5 stars:
1. **Daily Fertilizer**: Apply organic fertilizer purchased from the general store every morning.
2. **Rain Boost**: Rainy days provide a natural growth boost and slightly elevate the mutation odds of adjacent hybrid sprouts.
3. **Selective Breeding**: Always dig up lower-star blooms and replace them with higher-star offspring as parents to breed pure 5-star varieties.

## Crafting & Decorating Uses

Hybrid flowers serve multiple creative purposes across your Heartopia journey:
- **Custom Fabric Dyes**: Craft bright wardrobe dyes at the textile loom.
- **Floral Garlands & Arches**: Required blueprints for outdoor wedding pavilions and garden cafe furniture.
- **Premium Gifting**: High-star rare flowers grant massive friendship boosts when gifted to floral-loving town villagers like Sophie!
