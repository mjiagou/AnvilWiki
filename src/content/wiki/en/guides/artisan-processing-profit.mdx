## Understanding Artisan Goods & Passive Workshop Automation

In the early game of Heartopia, your income depends on active stamina expenditure—hoeing soil, swinging pickaxes, and reeling in fish. As your property expands, **Artisan Workshops** become your premier passive wealth generators.

By placing automated machines on your home plot, you convert raw crops, shorn sheep wool, and unrefined gemstone geodes into luxury goods. These machines work continuously while you sleep or explore, multiplying raw material values by **200% to 320%** with zero additional stamina drain.

## 3. The 3-Tier Workshop Layout (Zero-Fatigue Automation)

To maximize throughput without spending your entire day running across your estate, group your machines into a dedicated **Industrial Production Quarter**:

```
[ Plot Entrance ] ➔ [ 4x Preserve Jars ] ➔ [ 2x Jeweler Wheels ] ➔ [ 2x Looms ] ➔ [ Storage Chest ]
```

- **Machine Proximity**: Place all machines along a single exterior fence line on your home plot.
- **Chest Integration**: Place a large wooden storage chest at the center of the line stocked with bulk fruits, wool, and uncut gems.
- **Batch Queuing**: Queue 8 machines simultaneously in under 45 seconds right before logging off or heading out on a fishing excursion.

## Pro Artisan Workshop Layouts & Queue Optimization

Having tested hundreds of machine cycles across live server economies, here are essential guidelines to prevent idle bottlenecks:

### 1. The High-Base-Value Golden Rule
Preserve Jars multiply value on a percentage basis (+150%). Putting a 15-Gold Carrot in a jar yields 37 Gold (+22 Gold gain over 40 minutes). Putting a 120-Gold bunch of Grapes in the same jar yields 300 Gold (+180 Gold gain). **Never process low-tier field vegetables; reserve jars exclusively for Grapes, Strawberries, and Black Truffles!**

### 2. The Overnight Batch Cycle
Machines continue processing while you are logged off:
- Before logging off at night, clear all finished goods into your backpack.
- Reload every machine with your longest-duration recipes (such as **Oak Aging Casks** and **Artisan Grape Jams**).
- Upon logging in after the 6:00 AM UTC reset, you will be greeted by thousands of gold coins in completed luxury goods ready to sell directly to Albert Jr.

### 3. Synergies with Gourmet Cooking
Do not sell 100% of your artisan jams! High-tier recipes like Gourmet Pastries and Deluxe Glazes from our [Cooking Recipes Guide](/guides/cooking-recipes/) require artisan jams as primary ingredients, boosting final dish resale value to well over 3,000 Gold per plate.
