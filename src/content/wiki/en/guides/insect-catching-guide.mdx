## Unlocking the Bug Net & Naniwa's Questline

The Insect Catching hobby becomes available once you reach **Dream Guide (DG) Level 5**. 

To begin, locate **Naniwa** stationed near the windmill along the Cloverdale outskirts. After a brief introductory quest on recognizing insect rustles in the tall grass, she will hand you your first **Bubble Net**.

## Core Catching Mechanics: Stalking & Range Indicators

Unlike fishing, insect catching requires stealth and patience:

1. **Spot the Bug**: Watch for fluttering wings, crawling shadows on tree trunks, or buzzing ground markers.
2. **Engage Stealth Mode**: **Do not run.** Running or hopping immediately frightens common and rare insects away. Hold your sneak button to creep forward slowly.
3. **Line Up Your Arc**: Approach the bug from behind or the side to stay out of its direct line of sight.
4. **Swing on Green**: A circular reticle will appear over the target. Wait until the ring turns solid green, then tap your net swing button to catch.

```
Spot ➔ Sneak (No Sprinting) ➔ Align from Behind ➔ Swing on Green Reticle
```

## Net Upgrades & Vivarium Displays

As you level up your Insect Catching hobby, visit Naniwa's workshop to purchase upgraded nets:
- **Reinforced Net**: Increases swing range by 25%, reducing the chance of premature escapes.
- **Silk Capture Net**: Adds a wider catch angle and grants bonus star-quality odds when netting rare tree-dwelling beetles.

### What to Do with Caught Insects
- **Cloverdale Museum**: Donate the first copy of each species for milestone achievement rewards and cosmetic plaques.
- **Home Vivariums**: Place custom glass terrariums inside your house or garden plot to display living specimens.
- **Selling for Gold**: Surplus rare and epic insects fetch substantial payouts from travelling collectors!
