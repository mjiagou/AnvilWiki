## First 7 Days Starter Route in Heartopia

Your initial week in Heartopia sets the foundation for your economy, house expansion, and hobby levels.

> 🚀 **Looking for fast rank progression and system unlock levels?** Follow our dedicated [Heartopia Dream Guide (DG) Leveling Guide](/guides/dream-guide-leveling/) to clear daily commission XP caps and unlock cooking, bug nets, and two-story housing on a fast schedule.

### Day 1–2: Complete Story Milestones and Unlock Hobbies
Focus strictly on the main quests provided by the Town Mayor. This unlocks your starter plot, essential gardening tools (detailed in our [Flower Breeding & Gardening Guide](/guides/flower-breeding-gardening/)), and the fishing rod (see our [Heartopia Fishing Guide](/guides/fishing-guide/)). Don't forget to redeem active promo items from our [All Heartopia Codes](/codes/all-codes/) catalog.

### Day 3–4: Establish Your Daily Resource Loop
Begin each morning checking your town requests and the daily bulletin board. Gather wood, stone, and plant your first harvest of rapid-growth crops from the [Heartopia All Crops & Seeds List](/items/crop-seeds-list/).

### Day 5–7: Upgrade Tools and Expand Your House
Use accumulated gold coins to purchase tool upgrades at the workshop and initiate your first plot renovations (refer to our [Housing Expansion & Building Guide](/guides/housing-expansion-building/)). Need fast funding? Use the batch cooking strategies in our [Gold Making Guide](/guides/gold-making-guide/).

## Understanding the Daily Reset (6:00 AM UTC)

Always plan your routine around the daily server reset:
- **Shop Stocks**: Limited-edition furniture and seed stocks restock daily.
- **NPC Requests**: Complete resident favors to build friendship levels for exclusive decor blueprints — check the [Villager Friendship Guide](/guides/villager-friendship-gifts/) for preferred gifts.
- **Weather Forecast**: Check tomorrow's weather to prepare for rain-exclusive fish in our [Heartopia All Fish List](/items/fish-species-list/) and rare insects in our [Insect Catching Guide](/guides/insect-catching-guide/).
