## First 7 Days Starter Route in Heartopia

Your initial week in Heartopia sets the foundation for your economy, house expansion, and hobby levels.

### Day 1–2: Complete Story Milestones and Unlock Hobbies
Focus strictly on the main quests provided by the Town Mayor. This unlocks your starter plot, essential gardening tools, and the fishing rod.

### Day 3–4: Establish Your Daily Resource Loop
Begin each morning checking your town requests and the daily bulletin board. Gather wood, stone, and plant your first harvest of rapid-growth crops.

### Day 5–7: Upgrade Tools and Expand Your House
Use accumulated gold coins to purchase tool upgrades at the workshop. Upgraded tools consume less stamina and yield bonus rare materials.

## Understanding the Daily Reset (6:00 AM UTC)

Always plan your routine around the daily server reset:
- **Shop Stocks**: Limited-edition furniture and seed stocks restock daily.
- **NPC Requests**: Complete resident favors to build friendship levels for exclusive decor blueprints.
- **Weather Forecast**: Check tomorrow's weather to prepare for rain-exclusive fish and rare insect spawns.
