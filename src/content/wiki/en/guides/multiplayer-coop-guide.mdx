## 1. Unlocking Multiplayer: The Island Passport Quest

Multiplayer features become active once your character reaches **Dream Guide (DG) Level 6**.

### Setup Steps:
1. Speak with **Mayor Bob** outside Town Hall to receive the quest *A Window to the World*.
2. Head to the **Cloverdale Ferry Dock** and talk to Harbor Master Douglas to stamp your **Island Passport**.
3. Open your in-game phone, navigate to the **Friends App**, and locate your **9-Digit Player UID**.
4. You can invite friends directly via UID search, QR code scan, or by opening your island gates to all online friends.

## Pro Island Security & Party Synergy Tips

Having hosted community farming festivals across multiple servers, here are verified practices to maximize party benefits while keeping your estate secure:

### 1. The Party Cooking Assembly Line
When batch-producing dishes for Albert Jr., arrange your kitchen layout using our [Housing Expansion & Building Guide](/guides/housing-expansion-building/):
- Place 3 or 4 kitchen counters in a tight semi-circle on a central kitchen rug.
- Ensure all party members stand within the rug boundary to activate the **Shared Kitchen** icon above their avatars.
- Assign one player to supply dough while another loads ovens to maintain continuous production without leaving the buff radius.

### 2. Dual-Cast Fishing on Ocean Docks
During stormy nights, pair up on the southern wooden pier. When two players cast their bobbers within 3 tiles of each other:
- Biting ripples spawn almost immediately.
- The visual tension ring moves 20% slower, making difficult apex predators like the Storm Marlin much easier to reel in without breaking lines.

### 3. Guestbook Friendship Bonuses
Encourage every visitor to sign your **Front Yard Guestbook**. Every unique visitor signature grants both players +25 Friendship Points and a chance to receive 5 free Wishing Stars in the morning mail.
