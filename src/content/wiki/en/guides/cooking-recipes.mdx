## Unlocking Cooking & The Golden Rule of Profit

Cooking in Heartopia is the single most lucrative profession in the game. You unlock it at **Dream Guide (DG) Level 5** by speaking with **Massimo** at the Town Center Bistro.

> 💡 **Looking for complete economy strategies?** See our master [Heartopia Gold Making Guide](/guides/gold-making-guide/) to learn how batch cooking pairs with multi-stove setups to generate 50,000+ gold per hour.

> 💰 **The Golden Rule of Heartopia Economy**: **Never sell raw ingredients.** Cooking multiplies the base value of your harvests by 200% to 350%. Selling raw truffles or wheat is throwing away free coins.

## Best Recipes for Maximum Gold Profit

Sell your finished gourmet meals to **Albert Jr.** at the market square for top-tier payouts:

| Dish Name | Key Ingredients | Profit Tier | Why It's Top Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Black Truffle Pie** | Wheat, Foraged Black Truffles | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (S-Tier) | Huge return on investment. Foraging truffles daily turns your cheap wheat into pure gold. |
| **Grape Jam** | Grapes, Sugar | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (A-Tier) | Fast crop growth cycle with high batch yields on multiple kitchen stoves. |
| **Strawberry Jam** | Strawberries, Sugar | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (A-Tier) | Accessible early-game staple with reliable, steady profit margins. |
| **Ratatouille** | Tomatoes, Eggplant, Bell Pepper | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (A-Tier) | Uses standard farm vegetable crops to create high-value multi-star meals. |
| **Fish and Chips** | Catch of the day (Cod/Bass), Potato | ⭐⭐⭐ (B-Tier) | Excellent way to liquidate surplus common fish caught while hunting rare species. |

## Best Meals for Energy & Stamina Recovery

When you're out mining iron ore or chopping trees, running out of stamina slows your day down. Keep these quick snacks in your backpack:

- **Tomato Sauce**: Takes only 1 minute to cook. Cheap, easy to mass produce, and restores enough energy for continuous farm chores.
- **Vegetable Salad**: High stamina recovery per bite, perfect before heading out on long exploration or foraging loops.

## Chef Massimo Friendship & Multi-Stove Strategy

1. **Raise Massimo's Friendship**: Talk to Massimo daily and gift him favorite dishes. Reaching higher friendship tiers unlocks exclusive gourmet blueprints that cannot be purchased anywhere else.
2. **Deploy Multiple Stoves**: You can place up to 3–4 kitchen counters on your home plot. Cooking batches concurrently triples your gold-per-minute throughput!
