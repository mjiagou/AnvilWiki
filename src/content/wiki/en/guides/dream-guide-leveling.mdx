## Understanding the Dream Guide (DG) Progression System

In Heartopia, your **Dream Guide (DG) Level** represents your character's overall town rank. Unlike individual hobby levels (such as Fishing or Gardening), your DG rank acts as the master key that gates the entire game:
- Unlocking new crafting professions and tools
- Opening restricted island biomes and deep ocean charters
- Authorizing [House Expansions & Multi-Story Blueprints](/guides/housing-expansion-building/)
- Accessing high-yield money-making recipes

## How to Earn DG XP Fast: Daily Budget Breakdown

Many players fall into the trap of endlessly chopping softwood or catching sardines, wondering why their DG bar barely inches forward. **Basic gathering actions do NOT grant DG XP directly**—they only award hobby mastery! 

To level up your DG rank rapidly, focus strictly on structured reward channels:

```
Daily Commissions (800 XP) + First Harvest (200 XP) + Resident Favors (150 XP) 
= ~1,150 Guaranteed Daily XP
```

### 1. Daily Town Requests (Bulletin Board) — 800 XP / Day
Every morning at **6:00 AM UTC**, the town bulletin board outside Town Hall generates 8 commission requests:
- **Gold Requests (⭐⭐⭐)**: Award 150 DG XP each (requires crafted meals or rare fish).
- **Silver Requests (⭐⭐)**: Award 100 DG XP each (requires standard farm crops or timber).
- **Bronze Requests (⭐)**: Award 50 DG XP each (basic foraging hand-ins).
- *Pro Tip*: Always reroll requests demanding uncrafted raw ores or off-season fish. Complete requests using stockpiled crops from our [All Crops & Seeds List](/items/crop-seeds-list/).

### 2. Dream Journal Chapter Milestones — 300 to 500 XP Each
Accessible through your in-game phone, the Dream Journal features one-time achievement chapters (e.g. "Catch 10 Unique Freshwater Fish", "Craft 5 Kitchen Dishes"). These chapters are exempt from daily reset caps and should be used to push through level gaps.

### 3. Resident Social Favors — 150 XP / Day
Speaking with Cloverdale villagers and fulfilling their spontaneous daily mini-favors provides 50 DG XP per resident (capped at 3 daily). Review our [Heartopia Villager Friendship Guide](/guides/villager-friendship-gifts/) to align gift deliveries with daily chat loops.

## 3-Phase Speed Leveling Strategy

### Phase 1: The DG Level 5 Sprint (Days 1 to 3)
- **Primary Objective**: Unlock kitchen batch cooking and the insect net.
- **Route**:
  1. Complete tutorial story quests immediately until you receive the starter cabin.
  2. Plant continuous batches of Wheat (15-minute cycle) to complete early farming journal milestones.
  3. Speak with Chef Massimo at the Bistro as soon as you hit DG Lv5 to unlock your first kitchen stove, following our [Heartopia Cooking Recipes Guide](/guides/cooking-recipes/).
  4. Visit Naniwa at the windmill to claim your first net from the [Heartopia Insect Catching Guide](/guides/insect-catching-guide/).

### Phase 2: Mid-Tier Estate & Forestry (Days 4 to 7)
- **Primary Objective**: Hit DG Level 8 and DG Level 10.
- **Route**:
  1. Upgrade to a Copper Axe to clear ancient fallen logs blocking the deep forest clearings.
  2. Transition your farm plots from wheat to high-value Grapes and Strawberries.
  3. Complete Fisherman Hank's ocean charter quest at DG Lv10 to unlock deep sea fishing using our [Fish Species & Price List](/items/fish-species-list/).

### Phase 3: The Second-Story Villa Push (Days 8 to 10)
- **Primary Objective**: Reach DG Level 12 for the two-story house upgrade.
- **Route**:
  1. Stockpile 50x Hardwood and 20x Iron Ingots from daily quarry mining loops.
  2. Clear all remaining Tier 2 Dream Journal chapters.
  3. Claim your second-floor building permit from Mayor Bob at the Town Hall.

## Critical Progression Traps to Avoid

1. **Grinding Stamina for Zero DG XP**: Chopping 100 trees with no active commission or journal milestone burns all your daily energy without advancing your DG rank.
2. **Ignoring the 6:00 AM UTC Reset**: Incomplete bulletin board requests expire at server reset. Turn in completed requests before logging off!
3. **Hoarding Low-Tier Materials**: Store at least 30 of each basic crop and timber in your home storage chests so you can instantly complete morning bulletin requests upon logging in.
