## Overview of Island Housing & Real Estate

Your home plot in Heartopia is your personal creative sanctuary. Beginning with a modest single-room cabin, you can progressively expand your floor area, add multi-story lofts, and landscape surrounding outdoor gardens.

All major structural expansions are authorized by **Mayor Bob** at the Town Hall Real Estate desk.

## House Upgrade Tiers, Costs & Prerequisites

Plan your economy ahead — higher-tier expansions require substantial supplies of refined lumber and metal ingots. (Need quick coins to afford your next floor? Read our [Heartopia Gold Making Guide](/guides/gold-making-guide/) to farm 45,000+ gold per hour).

| House Tier | Unlocked Features | Gold Cost | Required Materials | Prerequisite DG Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 (Starter)** | Single studio room, basic yard plot | Free | Tutorial Questline | DG Level 1 |
| **Tier 2 (Cozy Cabin)** | +40% interior room space, outdoor patio | 5,000 Gold | 20x Softwood, 10x Stone | DG Level 4 |
| **Tier 3 (Suburban Home)** | Left & right separate rooms, expanded garden | 25,000 Gold | 35x Hardwood, 15x Copper Ingots | DG Level 8 |
| **Tier 4 (Two-Story Villa)** | **Second floor unlocked**, interior staircase | 80,000 Gold | 50x Hardwood, 20x Iron Ingots | DG Level 12 |
| **Tier 5 (Luxury Estate)** | Rooftop observatory, greenhouse zoning | 200,000 Gold | 100x Hardwood, 40x Iron Ingots | DG Level 16 |

## Unlocking the Second Floor (Tier 4)

Reaching **House Tier 4** transforms your building capabilities:
1. **Staircase Placement**: Choose between spiral staircases, rustic wooden stairs, or modern floating glass steps.
2. **Loft & Balcony Builds**: Open up two-story vaulted ceilings or fence off a private rooftop sun terrace overlooking the ocean.
3. **Zoning Rooms**: Dedicated space for customized themed rooms (e.g. Master Bedroom, Chef Kitchen, Alchemy Workshop, Vivarium Gallery).

## Pro Build Mode Decorating Tips

- **Free-Form Placement**: Use the grid snap toggle to shift from rigid tile locking to millimeter precision. Great for angling chairs around outdoor firepits.
- **Elevation Layering**: Use small stone risers and low wooden decks to create tiered split-level living rooms.
- **Lighting Ambience**: Place warm wall lanterns and fairy lights along fences to give your plot a magical glow during night cycles!
