## Why Friendship Matters in Heartopia

Building relationships with the quirky residents of Cloverdale and Heartopia is more than just social flavor. Raising your friendship tiers unlocks:
- **Exclusive Kitchen Recipes** (e.g. Gourmet dishes from Chef Massimo)
- **Unique Furniture Blueprints** (Rustic woodwork from the Carpenter)
- **Town Shop Discounts** (Reduced seed and tool upgrade prices)
- **Resident Wall Portraits** for your house gallery

## Pro Gifting Strategy & Daily Route

To avoid running all over the island aimlessly, follow an optimized **clockwise loop**:

```
Town Hall (Mayor Bob) ➔ Market (Albert Jr.) ➔ Bistro (Massimo) ➔ Gardens (Sophie) ➔ Windmill (Naniwa)
```

### Key Friendship Rules
1. **Birthday Multiplier**: Check the Town Hall calendar weekly. Gifting an NPC on their designated birthday grants **3x friendship points**. Save your highest-star loved items for these days!
2. **Quality Matters**: A 3-star or 5-star quality crop or dish grants bonus experience over basic 1-star items.
3. **Daily Conversations**: Don't just hand over a gift and run. Talk to each villager once per day to trigger dialogue choices that grant free baseline friendship points.
