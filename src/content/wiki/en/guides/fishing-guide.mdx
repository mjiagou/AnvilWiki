## Core Fishing Mechanics & Tension Control

Mastering fishing in Heartopia is all about patience and respecting line tension:

1. **Cast Your Line**: Aim slightly in front of swimming fish shadows. Hold your cast button to gauge distance.
2. **Watch the Bite**: Wait for the bobber to dip completely with a splash audio cue, then tap to hook.
3. **Control Tension**:
   - **White Ring**: Completely safe — hold the reel button to draw the fish closer.
   - **Yellow Ring**: Tension is rising — be ready to let go.
   - **Red Blinking Ring**: Dangerous tension — **release immediately** or your line will snap and the fish will escape.

```
Safe Reeling (White) ➔ Release on Warning (Yellow/Red) ➔ Resume (White)
```

## Spawn Schedules: Time & Weather Breakdown

Fish spawns are governed by your in-game clock and the weather forecast (viewable in the weather app on your in-game phone):

| Time Window | In-Game Hours | Typical Fish Behavior | Recommended Spots |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Early Morning** | 06:00 – 09:00 | High activity for freshwater species | Town River, Forest Ponds |
| **Daytime** | 09:00 – 18:00 | Common and uncommon daily fish | Central Lake, Coastal Pier |
| **Evening** | 18:00 – 21:00 | Increased rate of rare fish | Sunset Bay, Outer Cliffs |
| **Night Window** | 21:00 – 06:00 | **Peak window for Legendary & Deep Sea fish** | Lighthouse Reef, Dark Ponds |

### Weather Multipliers
- **Sunny / Clear**: Standard spawn table. Best for leveling up your fishing hobby baseline.
- **Rainy / Stormy**: **Spawn rates for Rare & Epic fish double**. Several exclusive species (such as the Storm Marlin) will *only* appear during downpours.
- **Rainbow After Rain**: High bonus luck for double catches and larger specimen stars.

## Pro Tips for Collecting All 90+ Species

1. **Check the Collections App**: On your in-game phone, open the Collections app to inspect the Fish Observation tab. Uncaught fish display environmental hints.
2. **Use Mermaid Fish Attractors**: Obtained from weekly codes (`HEARTOPIA2026`) or event shops. They dramatically slow down line tension spikes, making difficult fish much easier to reel in.
3. **Upgrade Your Rod**: Higher-grade rods purchased from the workshop increase your sweet spot duration and allow you to reel heavier deep-sea targets.
