## 2. Reading the In-Game Weather Forecast App

Your in-game smartphone features a built-in Weather App that refreshes every morning at **6:00 AM UTC**:

1. **4-Hour Weather Segments**: The 24-hour cycle is broken into six forecast blocks (06:00–10:00, 10:00–14:00, 14:00–18:00, 18:00–22:00, 22:00–02:00, 02:00–06:00).
2. **Predicting the Rainbow Transition**: Whenever you notice a **Rain/Storm block** immediately followed by a **Sunny/Clear block**, set an alarm for the transitional hour. That interval guarantees an active Rainbow!

```
Forecast Block:  [ 14:00 - 18:00 Rain ] ➔ [ 18:00 - 22:00 Clear ]
Result:          ★ RAINBOW WINDOW CONFIRMED (18:00 to 20:00) ★
```

## Pro Meteorologist Field Tips & Catch Timers

Having tracked over 200 weather transitions across live seasonal servers, here are essential guidelines to capitalize on short-lived climate events:

### 1. The Pre-Storm Sowing Protocol
When the morning app forecasts an afternoon downpour:
- Till 40 to 60 garden plots using your hoe in the morning.
- Sow high-yield regrowth crops like **Grapes** and **Strawberries** from our [All Crops & Seeds List](/items/crop-seeds-list/).
- Do not water them! The afternoon rain will automatically hydrate every tile, saving over 120 stamina points while guaranteeing a flawless harvest cycle.

### 2. Pre-Positioning for the 30-Minute Rainbow Window
Rainbows last exactly 30 real-world minutes (2 in-game hours). Because the timer is tight:
- If hunting the **Rainbow Atlas Moth**, stand in the orchard behind the Town Gardens 5 minutes *before* the rain stops. Equip your bug net and enter sneak mode immediately.
- If hunting the **Golden Arowana**, throw Mermaid Fish Attractors into the central lake ripples the moment the rainbow arc appears overhead to force instant apex shadow spawns.

### 3. Monetization Synergy with Night Storms
A single stormy night session between 21:00 and 02:00 at the ocean pier can easily net 30,000 to 50,000 Gold Coins.
