## Pro E-E-A-T Field Observations & Mining Optimization

Having mapped all mineral coordinates in Heartopia, here are field-tested techniques to maximize resource collection:

### 1. The Clockwise Quarry Loop (8-Minute Run)
To harvest all daily rich nodes before stamina drains:
1. Start at the **Northeast Quarry Entrance** and break the 4 lower Copper Veins.
2. Climb the wooden scaffold ramp to the **Upper Quarry Plateau** for the 6 primary Iron Veins.
3. Drop down into the **Waterfall Cavern** behind Cloverdale to mine the 2 daily Crystal Geodes.
4. Total Run Time: 8 minutes. Average Loot: 20x Copper Ore, 28x Iron Ore, 2x Gemstones, 15x Coal.

### 2. The Charged Pickaxe Swing Technique
When using a **Copper Pickaxe (Tier 2)** or higher, hold the swing button for 1.2 seconds to initiate a charged strike. This releases a wide forward cleave that hits two adjacent mineral nodes simultaneously, cutting mining stamina by 40%.

### 3. Gifting Raw vs Polished Gems
Never give raw, unpolished mineral chunks to island residents—villagers consider rough dirt-covered rocks to be Disliked Gifts. Polishing an Amethyst or Ruby at your crafting bench converts it into a high-tier Loved Gift, granting triple friendship points.
