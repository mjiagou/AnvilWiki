## Essential Materials Overview

Gathering materials efficiently is key to decorating your island plot, crafting furniture blueprints, and upgrading your tools in Heartopia.

### Core Materials Master Table

| Material | Type | Primary Source | Primary Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Softwood** | Lumber | Chopping standard pine & birch trees | Starter furniture, fences, wooden chests |
| **Hardwood** | Lumber | Chopping aged oak trees in the forest | House expansions, bridge blueprints, Tier 2 tools |
| **Copper Ore** | Mineral | Quarry stone nodes | Copper Ingots, Copper watering cans & axes |
| **Iron Ore** | Mineral | Deep quarry crystal veins | Iron Ingots, advanced machinery, multi-cook stoves |
| **Wheat** | Crop | Farming (seeded from general store) | Black Truffle Pies, bread, animal feed |
| **Strawberries** | Crop | Farming (purchased from seasonal vendor) | Strawberry Jam, fruit smoothies, gourmet tarts |
| **Wishing Stars** | Premium Currency | Promo codes, weekly benefits, daily streaks | Mermaid lures, limited-edition furniture gacha |

## Tool Upgrade Progression & Workshop Requirements

Upgrading your tools reduces the stamina consumed per swing and significantly increases the chance of harvesting bonus rare drops:

1. **Starter Tools (Wood)**: Provided during the town tutorial by the Mayor. Basic gathering speed.
2. **Copper Tools (Tier 2)**: Requires 5x Copper Ingots + 10x Softwood. Chops large stumps and breaks medium boulders.
3. **Iron Tools (Tier 3)**: Requires 8x Iron Ingots + 5x Hardwood. Breaks quarry crystal veins and clears ancient fallen logs blocking hidden trails.

## Rare Weather Collectibles

Keep a close eye on the in-game forecast app on your phone:
- **Rain & Downpours**: Search around water edges for glowing blue mushrooms and electric snails.
- **Nighttime Forest**: Fireflies and luminous beetles spawn between 20:00 and 04:00 in the deep woods.
