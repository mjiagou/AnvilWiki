## Essential Materials Overview

Gathering materials efficiently is key to decorating your island plot and expanding your wardrobe in Heartopia:

### Wood & Minerals
- **Softwood & Hardwood**: Chopped from various town trees using your axe. Required for virtually all wooden furniture blueprints.
- **Copper & Iron Ore**: Mined from mineral nodes in the quarry. Essential for Tier 2 and Tier 3 tool upgrades.

### Seeds & Crops
- **Wheat & Carrots**: Quick-harvest crops ideal for beginners. Used to cook stamina-restoring meals.
- **Strawberries & Roses**: Higher profit margin crops that require daily watering and fertilizer.

### Rare Collectibles
Keep an eye out during rainy weather for rare fish and glowing insects around the central lake. These sell for premium prices to the traveling collector.
