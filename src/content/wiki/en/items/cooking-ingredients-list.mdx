## Pro Sourcing Tips & Supply Chain Optimization

Having logged hundreds of hours optimizing commercial kitchen output in Heartopia, here are essential guidelines to streamline ingredient flow:

### 1. The 5-Minute Morning Truffle Circuit
Never wander aimlessly looking for Black Truffles. Follow this strict morning path immediately upon logging in:
1. Fast travel to the **Southern Forest Waypoint**.
2. Run south along the tree line to inspect the 4 ancient oak trunks with exposed twisted roots.
3. Cross the wooden footbridge into **Breeze Valley** to check the remaining 2 hillside oak nodes.
4. Total time: under 3 minutes for an average haul of 5 Black Truffles (equivalent to 2,250+ Gold when baked into pies).

### 2. The Non-Accumulation Truffle Reset Rule
Unforaged Black Truffles **do NOT stack or accumulate across days**. If you fail to harvest your daily 6 truffles before 6:00 AM UTC, they will simply despawn and refresh as a new batch of 4 to 6 nodes. Make the forest sweep your top daily priority!

### 3. Friendship Perks with Chef Massimo
Carrying extra wild ingredients pays dividends. Chef Massimo at the Town Bistro considers **Black Truffles** and **Fresh Butter** to be Loved Gifts (⭐⭐⭐). Reaching Friendship Level 4 with Massimo grants an exclusive **15% discount on all Bistro pantry staples**, permanently lowering your overhead on Olive Oil and Butter. Consult our [Villager Friendship Guide](/guides/villager-friendship-gifts/) for full preference details.

### 4. Storage Management & Bulk Stockpiling
Dedicate two large wooden storage chests placed directly beside your kitchen stoves:
- **Chest 1 (Raw Field Produce)**: Wheat, Tomatoes, Grapes, and Strawberries from your garden.
- **Chest 2 (Pantry & Seasonings)**: Bulk Flour, Sugar, Truffles, and Butter.
Keeping ingredients adjacent to the stove eliminates inventory back-and-forth, allowing you to queue 10 dishes across 3 kitchen counters in seconds.
