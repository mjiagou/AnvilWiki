## Shadow Size Recognition Guide

Saving your bait for target fish saves significant time:
- **Size 1 (Tiny)**: Minnows, Sardines, and small baitfish. Fast biting, low line tension.
- **Size 2–3 (Small/Medium)**: Trout, Carp, Snappers, and common food ingredients.
- **Size 4 (Large)**: Bass, Catfish, and valuable mid-tier collection milestones.
- **Size 5 (Gigantic)**: Sharks, Marlins, and Legendary specimens. Requires careful tension release on yellow/red alerts!
