## Pro E-E-A-T Stalking Mechanics & Field Tips

Having logged over 100 hours cataloging Heartopia fauna, here are our field-tested findings to minimize startled escapes:

### 1. The 3-Tile Detection Bubble
Every tree beetle possesses a 3-tile sensory radius. Stepping into this zone while sprinting causes an immediate flee. Always initiate sneak mode at **5 tiles away** from the trunk.

### 2. Reading Bug Body Language
- **Relaxed State**: Wings flutter slowly, antennas twitch at intervals. Safe to advance.
- **Alert State**: The insect freezes stiffly and raises its front limbs. **Halt your movement instantly.**
- **Recovery**: After 1.5 seconds of immobility, the insect resumes its relaxed cycle. You may creep forward again.

### 3. Gift Synergies with Island Residents
Do not sell all your catches immediately! Certain villagers highly cherish specific insect specimens:
- **Naniwa (Windmill Resident)**: Considers **Luminous Fireflies** and **Sawtooth Stag Beetles** to be Loved Gifts (⭐⭐⭐). Check our [Heartopia Villager Friendship & Gift Guide](/guides/villager-friendship-gifts/) for full point multipliers.
- **Sophie (Florist)**: Loves receiving **Peacock Butterflies** and **Honeybees** for her garden pollination projects.

### 4. Maximizing Star-Quality Specimen Values
Insects caught with higher-tier nets (such as the Silk Net and Master Net crafted using supplies from our [Essential Items & Materials Guide](/items/getting-started/)) have a 35% higher probability of spawning as 3-star, 4-star, or 5-star specimens. 5-star specimens fetch exactly **200% of the base retail value** when sold to Albert Jr. or visiting collectors.
